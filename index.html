    About me

    Music is therapeutic and I enjoy sharing the experience and energy it brings to people.

    As a teenager, I admired the art of mixing music, scratching, and discovering new sounds. As a DJ, it is my inspiration to pay homage to the forefathers of this art and continue to create sounds that inspire people to move. I believe music has the power to ease pain, bring joy, inspire, and most of all unite a collective. This is the reason why I love DJing and being able to create those unforgettable experiences.

    My favorite music genres are dancehall, afrobeat, and hip-hop, however, I listen to music from all over the world.
